physical properties|any characteristic of matter that can be observed or measured without changing the chemical identity or composition of the substance
isotopes|any of two or more species of atoms of the same chemical element that share the same number of protons
radioisotopes|an atom with an unstable nucleus that spontaneously emits energy in the form of radiation
electron affinity|the amount of energy released when an electron is added to a neutral atom or molecule in the gaseous state to form a negative ion
crystalline structures|a highly ordered, repeating three-dimensional arrangement of atoms, ions, or molecules in a solid material
Lewis structures|a simplified visual representation of valence electrons in a molecule
ionization energy|the minimum energy required to remove the most loosely bound electron from an isolated gaseous atom, ion, or molecule
atomic radius|a measure of the size of an atom
effective nuclear charge|the net positive charge experienced by an electron in a multi-electron atom
electronegativity|the tendency of an atom to attract shared electrons (or electron density) toward itself when part of a chemical bond
chemical reactions|the process in which one or more substances (reactants) are transformed into one or more different substances (products) through the breaking and forming of chemical bonds
